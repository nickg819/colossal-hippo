Privacy Policy

Effective Oct. 23, 2026

SeatSpy is an iPhone app made by Nick Gassmann. It keeps a read on the poker players you face. This page covers what happens to what you record in it.

Your Book Stays on Your Phone

The players you save, the words you pick to describe them, their types, their stats and the rest of your book are kept in a database inside SeatSpy’s own storage on your iPhone. Only SeatSpy can read it. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that holds your book. I cannot see what you record.

When Your Book Leaves Your Phone

SeatSpy never sends your book anywhere on its own. It leaves your phone in these cases only.

  • Your phone’s backup. If iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer is turned on, iOS includes SeatSpy’s data in it. Apple’s terms cover that backup.
  • Back up book. This saves your book as one file and opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you send it.
  • Report a bug, Request a feature and Share feedback. Each one opens an email to me in Mail. A bug report fills in the app version, your iPhone model and your iOS version. Share feedback fills in your answers to its questions. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and nothing from your book goes in unless you type it.

Checks for a Newer Version

SeatSpy goes online in two cases, and neither one sends anything from your book.

  • App updates. SeatSpy gets fixes and new content from Expo’s update service. It checks when the app opens and again after an hour away. Each check sends the app’s version, the update channel, the platform, the ID of the update you are running and a random ID that SeatSpy makes when it is installed. That ID is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your phone. If an update fails to start, the next check also sends the error message. Expo’s privacy policy covers what Expo keeps.
  • New TestFlight builds. The Home screen reads a small file on colossalhippo.com to see whether a newer build is in TestFlight. The request asks for that file and sends nothing else. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub’s privacy statement covers its server logs.

Both requests reach their servers with your IP address, as any internet connection does.

What SeatSpy Does Not Do

SeatSpy has no analytics, ads, crash reporting service or push notifications, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. It never asks for your camera, photos, location, contacts or microphone.

Apple

If you turn on Share With App Developers in iPhone Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements, Apple sends me crash reports and usage figures for SeatSpy. If you install SeatSpy through TestFlight, any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight also reach me. Apple’s privacy policy covers what Apple collects.

Deleting Your Data

Reset data in Settings clears the book and keeps a safety copy on your phone, so the reset can be undone. Deleting SeatSpy from your phone removes the book, the safety copies and everything else the app stored. Copies already in a backup, a file you shared or an email you sent stay where they are.
