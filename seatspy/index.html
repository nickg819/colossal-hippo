How it works, out of your paper notebook and into your phone

  1. Seat the Players

    Tap a seat to add someone from your book or a stranger. You always sit at the bottom middle, so the table reads the same way every session.

  2. Tag Their Type

    Twelve player types, from Calling Station to Maniac. Not sure yet? Quiz me asks what you have seen and ranks the likely types. Next player moves you around the table to the next seat with no type.

  3. Exploit Their Mistakes

    How to exploit them says when to bluff, when to bet for value and when to back off, for that player in particular.

  4. Keep the Book

    Every read stays with the player. The next time they sit down, you start where the last session left off.

SeatSpy starts in Simple mode, with three plain lines for each type: whether to bluff them, what their raise means and how to bet a good hand. Full detail adds sizing, preflop play and the rest of the card. To change modes during a session, use the Simple switch beside the table name.

Learn from the comfort of your home

The advice is only useful if you know what a float or a 3-bet is. The Glossary covers more than 90 poker terms, and the terms in the advice have a dotted line under them. Tap one to see what it means.

Quiz yourself deals ten cards a round. Each answer turns the card over, green when you are right and red when you miss. The questions change as you go: pick the meaning, name the term, true or false, fill the blank from a real line of advice, or type the term yourself.

Quiz progress keeps count of the terms you know, shows which groups you are weakest in and runs a round on just the terms you keep missing.

Built for the card room

Use it before a game and between hands. The whole book lives on your phone, so it works in a back room with no signal.

Incognito mode shows each tagged seat as a plain dot. Press and hold the table to see the types, and let go to hide them again.

When someone changes seats, hold their seat and drag it to the new one. Drop them on another player and the two swap.
