How It Works

  1. Seat the Table

    Tap a seat to add someone from your book or a stranger. You always sit at the bottom middle, so the table reads the same way every session.

  2. Tag Their Type

    Twelve player types, from Calling Station to Maniac. Not sure yet? Quiz me asks what you have seen and ranks the likely types.

  3. Play Them Right

    How to play them says when to bluff, when to bet for value and when to back off, for that player in particular.

  4. Keep the Book

    Every read stays with the player. The next time they sit down, you start where the last session left off.

Built for the Card Room

Use it before a game and between hands. The whole book lives on your phone, so it works in a back room with no signal.
